# Impeccable Design Context — Ophelia's Flowers

## Design Context

### Users

Small-scale hobbyist gardeners and dahlia enthusiasts purchasing dahlia tubers for home planting. They shop seasonally (late winter/spring) and are passionate about their craft — they know what they want and they're excited to grow. They browse a small catalog rather than search a large inventory. They buy with anticipation: these tubers will become the blooms in their garden. The checkout is a pre-order with offline payment follow-up, so trust and warmth in the ordering experience are critical.

### Brand Personality

**Words**: Abundant · Handmade · Alive

**Voice**: Unpretentious and personal, like a note tucked into a box of tubers from someone who genuinely loves what they grow. Not precious, not corporate — conversational but not casual.

**Emotional goal**: Excitement and abundance. A visitor should feel the urge to fill their garden, the visual equivalent of flipping through a seed catalog and wanting one of everything. The structural restraint of the Elegant Brutalist aesthetic is the _container_ for that energy — the grid holds it, the typography anchors it, but the photography and the names of the varieties are allowed to be vivid and full of life.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Direction**: Elegant Brutalism — a refined Playfair Display serif set against an undecorated structural grid. The warmth comes entirely from the photography and the cream background, not from decorative UI elements.

**Color palette** (all in use):

- `cream` (`#faf8f5`) — page background, warm not clinical
- `ink` (`#1a1a1a`) — primary text, borders, buttons
- `stone-500` (`#78716c`) — muted secondary text
- `stone-300` (`#d6d3d1`) — dividers, disabled states
- `dahlia-wine` (`#722f37`) — low-stock warnings, sale prices, error states
- `dahlia-blush` (`#d4a0a0`) — soft hover tints
- `botanical` (`#4a6741`) — in-stock status, success states

**Theme**: Light only. No dark mode.

**Anti-references** — what this should never look like:

- Generic Shopify/Etsy template: too homogenous, no personality, indistinguishable from a hundred other shops
- Overcrowded garden-centre aesthetic: too many thumbnails, competing banners, discount stickers, cluttered navs
- Sterile minimal tech startup: cold, clinical white, no warmth or life

**Typography**:

- Headings: `Playfair Display` (Google Fonts, weight 400 and 700). Used for `h1`, `h2`, and display copy.
- Body: System font stack (`system-ui`, `-apple-system`, etc.)
- Navigation and labels: `text-xs uppercase tracking-widest` — the brutalist second rhythm

**Motion**: Functional only. Hover color transitions. Cart badge pulse (150ms). View Transitions crossfade (200ms). No scroll effects, no parallax, no decorative animation.

### Design Principles

1. **The photography is the product.** Every layout decision should prioritize putting high-quality dahlia imagery front and centre. The UI is a quiet frame, not a decorated stage.

2. **Excitement through content, restraint through structure.** The grid, typography, and color palette are deliberately minimal. The excitement comes from the variety names, the bloom photography, and the anticipation of planting — not from UI flourishes.

3. **Built, not decorated.** No rounded corners, drop shadows, or gradient fills. Borders are structural. Whitespace is intentional. The layout should look made by someone who cares about craft.

4. **Honest and personal.** This is a family farm named after a child. The copy, the About page, and the overall tone should feel like a real person is behind it — not a faceless brand. Avoid marketing clichés.

5. **WCAG AA is the floor, not the ceiling.** Color contrast, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, and accessible form patterns are non-negotiable. Every new component is tested before shipping.
